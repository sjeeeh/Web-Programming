List in HTML

In HTML, there are three primary types of lists: Unordered (bulleted), Ordered (numbered), and Description (dictionary style).
Every basic list uses a wrapping parent tag to define the type, and individual list items inside.

Unordered List

Used when the order of the items does not matter. By default, items are marked with bullet points.

  • Chilled Water
  • Black Coffee
  • Green Tea

Ordered List

Used when items follow a specific sequence. By default, items are marked with numbers.

  1. Boil water
  2. Add tea bag
  3. Serve hot

Description List

Used for terms and their corresponding definitions, like a glossary or dictionary.
